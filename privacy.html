Privacy policy

TripFuelCost does not require an account and does not store the locations or vehicles you enter on our servers.

Third-party services

To calculate your trip, your browser sends the text you type to these services:

  • Photon (location search)
  • Valhalla by FOSSGIS and OSRM (driving distance and time)
  • FuelEconomy.gov (vehicle data and fuel prices)
  • OpenStreetMap (map tiles)
  • Google Maps Platform (fuel prices at gas stations near your starting point)
